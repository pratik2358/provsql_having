\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented a general semantics for \sql{HAVING} queries that
applies to provenance in any commutative m-semiring, giving a value to
the formal comparison expressions of~\cite{amsterdamer2011provenance} in
every such semiring. It agrees with the provenance of the self-join
rewriting in m-semirings that are absorptive and where times distributes
over monus, in particular in the Boolean-function semiring needed for
probability evaluation; in other semirings the two differ, unavoidably,
since outside Boolean provenance the way a query is written changes its
provenance. Algorithms for specific aggregate functions and comparison
operators, together with experiments, show the feasibility of the
approach, which yields the first end-to-end provenance-aware
implementation of selection on aggregate values. A natural next step is
to combine this work with the query-shape tractability results
of~\cite{re2009trichotomy}, to guarantee polynomial-time probabilistic
evaluation of safe \sql{HAVING} queries.
